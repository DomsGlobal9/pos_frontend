## 1. Keep it

1. Put the items on the bill as usual.
2. **More → Keep for customer**. Add the customer if you have not (a kept order always belongs to someone).
3. In **Note**, write what is being done: *Fall and pico, blouse to be stitched*.
4. In **Collect on**, choose when they will come.
5. **Next: advance** — take what they pay now. Nothing is fine too; type 0 or leave it empty.
6. **Keep for customer**.

The bill prints **KEPT FOR COLLECTION** with the note, the date and the **Balance due**. It is their claim
ticket.

## 2. The Orders screen

**Orders** at the bottom, with tabs:

- **Waiting** — still at the tailor. The soonest collection date is on top; a late one is flagged.
- **Ready** — ready to collect.
- **Due** — money still owed (kept orders, credit sales, and any bill whose UPI never arrived).
- **Complete** — handed over and paid.

## 3. When it is ready

Open the order, press **Mark ready**, then **WhatsApp: it's ready** — the message is written for you,
with what is still to pay. You press send on your own phone.

## 4. Hand it over

Open the order, **Take payment** for the rest, then **Hand over**. The bill now says **COLLECTED**.

:::faq They want it but have not paid the rest
**Hand over** warns *₹799 still owed*. If they are a trusted regular, say yes: it goes home and stays under
**Due**. See [Collect what is owed](/help/orders/collect-what-is-owed).
:::

:::faq The customer cancels before collecting
Use **Return** on the bill. Collect anything owed first; the till says so.
:::
