## 1. Find it

**Orders → Due**, or the customer's card under **Customers**, or search the name or bill number in
**Orders**.

## 2. Remind them

On the order, **WhatsApp: remind about ₹2,000** opens WhatsApp with the message written: the amount, the
bill number and a link to the bill. You press send.

## 3. Take the money

1. Press **Take payment**.
2. Cash, UPI or card, the full amount or part of it. More than is owed is refused.
3. Press **Take payment** in the box.

It goes into the drawer that is open now, on today's date. When nothing is left, the order says
**Paid in full** and the bill **CREDIT PAID IN FULL**.

:::note Part payments
₹500 now and ₹1,500 next week is fine: each one is recorded, and the order shows what is still owed.
:::

:::faq Points or store credit for a balance?
A balance is collected in money — cash, UPI or card. Points and store credit pay at the time of sale.
:::
