## 1. Make the credit sale

1. Put the items on the bill.
2. **More → Sell on credit**. Add the customer if you have not — owed money always belongs to someone.
3. Take what they pay today, if anything. **Nothing is fine too**: leave it empty or type 0. The screen
   shows **Owed on credit**.
4. Press **Sell on credit**.

![The credit sale payment box with ₹300 paid now. Owed on credit is marked 1 and Sell on credit 2.](1-owed.webp "Nothing paid now is fine too.")
5. A cashier sees **Manager approval needed** — the manager types their PIN, unless the customer has
   **credit allowed** and this stays within it (see below). A manager or owner goes straight through.


![Manager approval needed. Why is marked 1, Manager PIN 2 and Approve 3.](2-approval.webp "The cashier stays signed in.")
The bill prints **ON CREDIT** and **Balance due**.

:::note Example
A ₹2,700 saree, ₹700 paid in cash now: *Owed on credit ₹2,000*. Points: 7 now, 20 more when the ₹2,000
comes in.
:::

## 2. Afterwards

The bill is under **Orders → Due**, and on the customer's card. Remind them on WhatsApp from the order,
and take the money when they come: [Collect what is owed](/help/orders/collect-what-is-owed).

When it is fully paid, the bill says **CREDIT PAID IN FULL**. If they will never pay:
[Write off](/help/orders/write-off).

## 3. Credit allowed, for regulars

On the customer's page (**Customers →** their name), a manager or the owner presses **Change** beside
**Credit allowed** and types how much they may owe in all, for example 5,000. Leave it empty to ask a
manager every time.

- Within it, a cashier sells on credit, or hands over a kept order with money owed, without a manager.
- Over it, the manager is asked as before, and is told the limit.
- At the till, adding the customer shows **Owes ₹1,000 · ₹4,000 credit left**.

:::faq "A manager needs to approve selling on credit"
The shop is lending money, so a cashier needs a manager's yes, unless the customer has credit allowed
and this stays within it. Paying in full "on credit" lends nothing, so it needs nobody.
:::
