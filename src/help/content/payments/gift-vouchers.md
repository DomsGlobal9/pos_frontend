## Sell one

1. **More → Sell a gift voucher**.
2. Type the amount, and if they like, who it is from and their phone.
3. **Take ₹…**, then take the money in cash, UPI or card, for certain. A voucher is as good as cash, so it
   cannot be "checked later".
4. The slip shows the code (like **GV-7KQ2-9XMA**), a QR, the amount and how long it is valid. **Print** it
   for the customer.

If Inventory does not answer, no voucher is made. Press **Sell** again: the same voucher comes back,
never two. Do not give the money back while you wait.

## Take one as payment

1. **Take payment → Gift voucher**.
2. Type the code (small letters and spaces are fine) and press **Check**. The box says what is on it,
   whose it was and till when, and fills in what can be used on this bill.
3. Take the rest another way if the voucher does not cover it. Two vouchers on one bill are fine.

An expired, blocked or used-up voucher says so, and cannot be taken.

## Returns

What a voucher paid goes **back onto the voucher**, never as cash: choose **Back onto the gift voucher**.
An expired voucher gets 30 more days.

:::note Example
A ₹5,000 voucher pays ₹3,000 of a saree. ₹2,000 stays on the code for next time. If the saree comes back,
₹3,000 goes back onto the same voucher.
:::

:::faq Where is the money in the day close?
Under **Gift vouchers**, apart from sales: the shop owes it in goods. Cash taken for vouchers is in the
drawer, and the shift and day close count it.
:::

:::faq Gift voucher is not offered
The shop must be connected to Inventory and online, and gift vouchers switched on in Inventory →
Settings → Gift vouchers.
:::
