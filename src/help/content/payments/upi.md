## 1. The customer pays

Press **Take payment**, choose **UPI**. The screen shows the shop's own UPI QR for that exact amount
(*Scan to pay ₹2,700*). The customer scans it with any UPI app — or pays to your soundbox or printed QR.

## 2. Type the UTR

On the customer's phone, the payment shows a **12-digit number** (UTR, UPI reference or transaction ID).
Type it in [[2]] **UPI reference** and press **Complete sale**.


![The payment box on UPI. UPI is marked 1, UPI reference 2 and Not confirmed yet 3. A QR for the exact amount is shown.](1-upi.webp "The QR already has the amount in it.")

[[1]] **UPI**, [[2]] the 12-digit UTR, [[3]] **Not confirmed yet** when it has not shown up.

:::tip Why the UTR?
It is the proof the money came. It goes on the bill, reaches Inventory, and the till stops the same UTR
being used for two bills.
:::

## Not seen it yet?

The customer shows "paid" but your phone has not pinged:

1. Tick **Not confirmed yet**.
2. **Complete sale**. The customer can go.

The payment waits on **More → Payments to check** until a manager ticks it off against the bank. See
[Payments to check](/help/payments/payments-to-check).

## UPI that confirms itself

When the owner has connected Razorpay in Inventory, **UPI** shows a Razorpay QR instead and the till ticks
itself the moment the money lands — no UTR to type. It costs the shop Razorpay's fee per payment. See
Inventory's guide: [Take payments online](https://inventory.scaleezy.com/help/online-shop/take-payments-online).

## Common problems

:::faq "Type the 12-digit UTR"
A UTR has exactly 12 digits. Spaces are fine; letters are not. The number starting with "T" in some apps
is the app's own reference — look for the 12-digit one.
:::

:::faq "That UPI reference is already on bill INV/…"
Check the number with the customer. A manager can allow it if one payment truly paid for both bills.
:::
