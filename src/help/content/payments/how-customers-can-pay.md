The customer can pay any way they like. Cash, UPI and card are on for every shop; points and store
credit come with the [Inventory link](/help/behind/inventory-link).

| How they pay | What you do on the till | How the till keeps it honest |
|---|---|---|
| **Cash** | **Cash**, then **Exact** or the notes handed over. The till shows the change. | Counted in the drawer when the shift closes. |
| **UPI** — any app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM…) to the shop's QR or soundbox | **UPI**, and type the **12-digit UTR** from the customer's phone. | A UTR already on another bill is stopped. |
| **UPI that confirms itself** (when the owner has connected Razorpay in Inventory) | Show the QR on the screen; the till ticks itself when paid. | Checked with Razorpay before the bill is made. |
| **Card on any swiping machine** (Pine Labs, Paytm, PhonePe, Ezetap, a bank's machine…) | Take the card on the machine as always. Then **Card**, and type the **last 4 digits** and the **approval code** from the slip. | The same slip cannot be used for two bills. |
| **Card EMI, Amex, a wallet** run on that machine | Same as a card: it is a card payment on the slip. | — |
| **Points** | **Points** — the till shows how much they can use. | Checked with Inventory at that moment. |
| **Store credit** | Shown in the payment box when they have some. | Checked with Inventory at that moment. |

## Two ways at once

₹1,000 in cash and the rest on UPI: fill the first, press **Split — add another payment**, fill the
second. The two must add up to the bill.

## Not sure the UPI came?

Tick **Not confirmed yet**. The bill is made, the customer can go, and the payment waits on
[Payments to check](/help/payments/payments-to-check) to be ticked off against the bank. Never ask the
customer to pay again.

:::note The swiping machine is not connected to the till
It does not need to be. You type the amount on the machine as always; the till only records the slip's
last 4 digits and approval code. Any brand, any bank, and changing the machine changes nothing here.
:::

## Common problems

:::faq "That UPI reference is already on bill INV/…"
Usually a wrong number: check it with the customer. If one payment really paid for two bills, a manager
can allow it. See [Manager approval](/help/setup/manager-approval).
:::

:::faq Points or store credit "can't be used right now"
They are kept by Inventory, and the till could not reach it, or the till is offline. Take that part
another way.
:::

:::faq Cheques?
The till does not take cheques.
:::
