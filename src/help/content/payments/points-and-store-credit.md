## Paying with points or store credit

With a customer on the bill, the payment box shows what they can use, for example *584 points worth
₹584 — needs 100 points to start*. Choose **Points** (or store credit) and the amount.

- They need a customer on the bill.
- They are checked with Inventory at that moment, so they cannot be used offline.
- Some shops allow points only up to part of a bill; the till says so.

## Earning points

Points are earned on what was **paid**. On a credit (udhaar) bill, the unpaid part earns nothing until it
is collected: a ₹2,700 bill with ₹700 paid earns 7 points at the sale, and 20 more when the ₹2,000 comes
in.

## On the bill

Points earned and the new balance print once Inventory has the bill — usually seconds. The first paper
copy may not have them; the online bill and every reprint do.

How many points a rupee earns, and what a point is worth, are set in Inventory. See Inventory's guide:
[Loyalty points](https://inventory.scaleezy.com/help/customers/loyalty-points).

## Returns

Points spent on something that comes back go back as points. A return of something bought with store
credit gives store credit, never cash.
