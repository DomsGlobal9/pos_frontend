When a cashier tries one of these, the till shows **Manager approval needed** with what is being asked.
The manager writes or checks **Why**, types their PIN in **Manager PIN** and presses **Approve**. The
cashier stays signed in and the bill stays as it was. The approval is recorded with both names.


![Manager approval needed. Why is marked 1, Manager PIN 2 and Approve 3.](1-approval.webp "The cashier stays signed in.")

| What | Who can do it alone |
|---|---|
| A discount above the shop's limit | Managers, owner |
| Changing an item's price | Managers, owner |
| Any return or refund | Managers, owner |
| A return after the return window | Managers, owner |
| Taking cash out of the drawer | Managers, owner |
| Marking a UPI as never arrived | Managers, owner |
| Selling on credit (udhaar) | Managers, owner |
| Allowing a UPI or card reference already on another bill | Managers, owner |
| Writing off what a customer owes | Managers, owner |
| Changing a business customer's saved GSTIN | Managers, owner (no approval: a cashier simply cannot) |

A manager doing these at the counter themselves goes straight through — no second manager is needed — and
it is still recorded.

:::note The reason is asked once
When the cashier already wrote why (for example on a write-off), it is filled in for the manager.
:::

:::faq "That person is not allowed to approve …"
Only someone whose role allows it can approve: a manager or the owner. A cashier's PIN is refused.
:::

:::faq Where is it all recorded?
**More → Activity** lists every approval with who asked, who said yes, and why. Reports count them.
:::
