**More → Staff**.

## Add a person

1. Press **Add person**.
2. Type their **Name** and choose their role:
   - **Cashier** — makes bills; needs a manager for the things in [What needs a manager](/help/setup/manager-approval).
   - **Manager** — everything a cashier does, and approves with their PIN.
   - **Owner** — everything.
3. Give a **4-digit PIN** — they use it to take the till.
4. For owners and managers only, an email or phone and a **Password** let them open a new counter.
5. Press **Save**. *They can now take the till with their PIN.*

Everyone with a PIN appears on **Who's at the till?** and in **Served by**.

## A forgotten PIN

A PIN cannot be read back by anyone. Open the person, type a **New PIN**, **Save**.

## Someone left

Press **Remove**. They can no longer take the till; their old bills keep their name. **Bring back** undoes
it.
