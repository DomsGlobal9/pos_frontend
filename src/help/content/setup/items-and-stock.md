:::note Connected to Inventory?
Then items and stock are kept in Inventory, and this screen says so. Everything below is for a shop
using the till on its own.
:::

## Add or correct an item

**More → Items → Add item**. Type the code on the tag, the name, the price (tax included) and the GST
rate. *How many* is the count to start from. A code that is already there is **corrected**, not
added twice: that is how you fix a price.

## Stock: receive, adjust, count

Press **Stock** on an item. Its stock book opens under it.

- **Received**: a parcel came in. Type how many, and the supplier or bill number in the note. The
  till shows what the count will be before you save.
- **Damaged / lost / found**: choose why, and how many. Damaged, lost or sent back takes the pieces
  off; found adds them. *Other* needs a few words on why.
- **Counted**: you counted the shelf. Type what is there; the till says how far off it was and sets
  the count to it.

Every change goes in the **stock book**: what, how many, the count before and after, the note, and
who did it. It is never edited or deleted. It also says how many sold since the last change by hand.

:::note Example
3 left. A parcel of 12 comes in: **Received 12**, note *Kanchi Silks bill 4471* → 15. Two are stained:
**Damaged 2** → 13. At night you count 12 on the shelf: **Counted 12** → the book shows −1.
:::

## Low and out of stock

At the top of the list: **All**, **Low** and **Out of stock**, each with how many. *Low means 2 or
fewer* until you press **Change** and type your own number.

## Common problems

:::faq The count went below what I expected
Each sale takes its pieces off, and each return puts them back, on their own. Open **Stock** on the item:
the book shows every change by hand and how many sold since the last one.
:::

:::faq A cashier cannot see Items
Items and stock are the owner's. A cashier sells; the owner sets the list and the counts.
:::
