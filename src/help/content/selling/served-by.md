In many shops the person who shows the sarees is not the person at the till. **Served by** records who
served, for incentives. The **Cashier** on the bill is still whoever took the money.

## 1. Choose who served

On **Sell**, under the customer, choose a name in [[1]] **Served by**. The list is your staff with a PIN.


![The sell screen with Served by marked 1 and a salesperson chosen.](1-served-by.webp "The list is your staff with a PIN.")

- It starts at **Nobody chosen**, and you can leave it there.
- It stays if the page reloads, and goes with a [parked bill](/help/selling/park-a-bill).
- **Next sale** starts again at **Nobody chosen**.

## 2. On the bill

The bill and its PDF print **Served by** with the name, under **Cashier**. The name is kept as it was
that day: renaming or removing someone later changes no old bill.

## 3. For incentives

The owner and managers see **Reports → By salesperson**: each person's bills, sales, returns on their
bills, net, and net **before GST**. See [Sales by salesperson](/help/reports/sales-by-salesperson).

:::faq Someone left the shop and an old offline bill had their name
The sale still goes through, naming nobody, and the cashier is told.
:::
