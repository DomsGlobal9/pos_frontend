## Offers (automatic)

Offers are set up in Inventory. When an item is on offer, the till takes it off by itself and shows
*offer −₹300* under the line. Nothing to press. If Inventory cannot be reached, the till says *Offers could
not be checked* and sells at the normal price — never refuses the bill. How offers are set up is in
Inventory's guide: [How offers work](https://inventory.scaleezy.com/help/offers/how-offers-work).

## Coupon code

**More → Coupon code**, type the code. The till says **Code … applied**, or why it was not accepted.

## A discount on the bill

**More → Discount**, type the amount or percent. Up to the shop's limit (set in Inventory's
[Till rules](https://inventory.scaleezy.com/help/offers/till-rules)), a cashier gives it alone. Above it, the till asks for a manager's PIN with a reason. See
[Manager approval](/help/setup/manager-approval).

## Change the price of one item

Tap the price on the line (**Change the price of …**) and type the new price. A cashier always needs a
manager for this; a manager or owner can do it themselves. Reports count every changed price.

:::note Example
A ₹3,000 saree with a ₹300 offer, and a 10% discount in a shop whose limit is 10%: offer −₹300, discount −₹270,
the customer pays ₹2,430. The GST is worked out on what was charged.
:::
