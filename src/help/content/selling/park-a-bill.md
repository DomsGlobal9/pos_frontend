## 1. Park it

**More → Park this bill**. The till says *Parked as "…"* and the screen is clear for the next customer.
The customer, the salesperson and any discount go with it.

## 2. Bring it back

**More → Parked bills**, tap the bill. It comes back exactly as it was.

:::tip One bill at a time
A parked bill comes back only to an empty till: finish or park the one on the screen first. The till
says *Park or clear the bill on the till first.*
:::

:::faq Two cashiers tapped the same parked bill
Only one gets it. The other is told it was taken a moment earlier.
:::
