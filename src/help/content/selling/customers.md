## Add a customer at the till

1. On **Sell**, press **Add customer**.
2. Type their 10-digit mobile number and press Enter.
3. Someone known: press **Use** with their name. Someone new: type a name if you like and press **Add and use**.

The same number is always the same customer, even if two counters add them at once.

:::note Never needed for a cash sale
A customer is optional for an ordinary sale. It is needed for points, store credit, kept orders,
credit (udhaar) and business bills.
:::

## Find a customer

**Customers** at the bottom. Type a name or part of a number. Each customer shows their visits, what they
have spent, their recent bills, and what they owe.

## Business details (GSTIN and address)

For a customer who wants a GST bill in their business's name: open them, **Change business details**,
fill **Business name**, **GSTIN** and **Address**, **Save**. From then on their bills print **Bill to**
with these. See [Business bills](/help/gst/business-bills).

- Anyone can **add** a GSTIN where there was none.
- **Changing or clearing** a GSTIN already saved needs a manager or the owner: *Changing a business's GSTIN
  needs a manager.* A wrong GSTIN would send their GST credit to someone else.
- Old bills keep the details they were issued with.

## Common problems

:::faq "That GSTIN looks mistyped"
The till checks the GSTIN's own check letter. One wrong character is caught. Copy it again from the
customer's GST certificate.
:::

:::faq "Add the business address too"
A business bill must show the buyer's address, so a GSTIN cannot be saved without one.
:::
