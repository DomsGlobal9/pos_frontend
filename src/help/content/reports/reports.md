**More → Reports**. Choose the days at the top — today, or any period up to 92 days.


![The Reports page for today: sales, how it was paid, cashiers, GST and more.](1-reports.webp "Choose the days at the top.")

## What is on it

- **Sales** — bills, net sales, average bill, discounts and offers, returns.
- **How it was paid** — cash, UPI, card, points… Below it: what is still **being checked**, what **never
  arrived**, and what was **written off as never paid** — none of these is money taken.
- **By cashier**, **By counter**, **By salesperson**.
- **Returns and discounts** — how many, why, changed prices, and what managers approved (*Sold on credit*,
  *Balances written off*…).
- **GST** — by rate: taxable value, CGST, SGST, IGST, after credit notes.
- **Cash counts** — every shift's count and difference.
- **Money owed** — every bill still owing, oldest first.
- **Day closes** and **Top sellers**.
- **Every item sold**: press **See every item sold** for every item in the period, sold less returned,
  with colour, size and what is left. Type to find one; **By design** adds a design's colours and sizes
  together. Owner and managers only.

:::note A cashier's view
A cashier sees **Your sales today**: only their own bills, today. The full reports are for managers and the
owner.
:::

Every figure is added up from the bills themselves, so the cashiers add up to the whole, and the GST lines
add up to the bills.
