## 1. Save their business details once

**Customers** → the customer → **Change business details** → **Business name**, **GSTIN**, **Address** →
**Save**. The till checks the GSTIN is real (one wrong letter is caught) and needs the address with it.

## 2. Bill as usual

Add the customer to the bill. The lookup shows *Business · GSTIN 27AAPFU0939F1ZV*, so you know whom the
bill is made out to.

The tax invoice prints:

> **Bill to** · Lakshmi Boutique · 4 Market Road, Pune 411001 · GSTIN 27AAPFU0939F1ZV

## Frozen on the bill

The buyer is kept on the bill as it was that day. Changing the customer's details later never changes a
bill already given — a credit note against it carries the same buyer.

:::note Only on a tax invoice
A shop with no GSTIN, or on the composition scheme, prints no Bill to: there is no GST for the buyer to
claim.
:::

:::faq "Changing a business's GSTIN needs a manager"
A cashier can add a GSTIN, not change one already saved: a swapped GSTIN would send the business's GST
credit to someone else.
:::
