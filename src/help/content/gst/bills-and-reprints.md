## 1. Find it

**More → Bills**. Search by bill number (the last digits are enough), customer name or phone, and narrow
by when or how it was paid. Or scan the QR printed on the bill.

## 2. Print, PDF or WhatsApp

Open the bill:

- **Print** — on whatever printer the counter has (80 mm or A4).
- **PDF** — a file to save or attach.
- **Share** — opens WhatsApp with a thank-you message and the bill's link (when there is a customer).

The link opens the bill on the customer's own phone, any time — without your till.

:::note Reprints say DUPLICATE
Every copy after the first prints **DUPLICATE · COPY 2** (3, 4…) at the top, so two identical bills never
go round for the same sale.
:::

## Points on a reprint

Points earned and the balance appear once Inventory has the bill (seconds). The first paper copy may not
have them; a reprint and the online bill do.

## Status on a reprint

A kept or credit bill says where things stand **now**: **ON CREDIT** while owed, **CREDIT PAID IN FULL**
once paid, **COLLECTED** once handed over, **BALANCE WRITTEN OFF** if written off.
