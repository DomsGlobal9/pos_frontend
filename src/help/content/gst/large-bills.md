## What you see

When a bill reaches ₹50,000 before GST, a line appears above the total:

> *Rs 50,000 or more: GST rules ask for the customer's name and address on this bill.* **Add customer**


![A bill over ₹50,000. The GST note is marked 1 and its Add customer button 2.](1-notice.webp "Take payment still works.")

## 1. Add their name and address

1. Press **Add customer** (or **Add them** if a customer is already on the bill).
2. Type their number. A new customer: type their **name** and **address**. A known one missing either: only
   what is missing is asked for, then **Save and use**.

The line goes away. The bill prints **Bill to** with their name, the address line by line, and the state:

> **Bill to** · Meena Rao · 12 Temple Street · Madurai 625001 · **State Tamil Nadu (33)**

The state is the shop's own: a counter sale is supplied where the shop is.

## The customer will not give them

**Take payment** still works. The sale goes through, and the cashier is told the bill went without them.

:::note Only on a tax invoice
A shop with no GSTIN, or on the composition scheme, never sees this.
:::
