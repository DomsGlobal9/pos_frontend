The till has two locks:

- **The counter** is opened once, by the owner or a manager, with their email or phone and password. It
  stays open on that computer or tablet until someone closes it.
- **The person** takes the till with their own 4-digit PIN. Every bill, drawer count and approval carries
  that person's name.

## 1. Open a new counter

1. On the counter's computer or tablet, open the till's address.
2. Type your **Email or phone** and **Password**.
3. Press **Open the till**.

Only an owner or a manager with a password can do this. Cashiers have only a PIN.

## 2. Take the till

The screen now says **Who's at the till?** with everyone's name.

1. Tap [[1]] your name.
2. Type your 4-digit PIN.

![Who's at the till? with everyone's name. Dev cashier is marked 1.](1-who.webp "Close the till on this device is at the bottom.")

## 3. Hand over to the next person

Tap **Switch** next to your name at the top. The next person taps their name and types their PIN. A bill
that was being made stays on the screen exactly as it was.

## Close the till on this device

On the **Who's at the till?** screen, **Close the till on this device** locks the counter itself. It then
needs the password again. Use it when a tablet is taken home or the counter is moved.

## Common problems

:::faq I forgot my PIN
Nobody can read a PIN back. Ask the owner or a manager to set a new one in **More → Staff**.
:::

:::faq The till went back to "Who's at the till?" on its own
A person's turn ends after a while for safety, or when someone pressed **Switch**. Take it again with
your PIN. Nothing on the bill is lost.
:::
