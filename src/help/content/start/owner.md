The owner can do everything on the till. Most of it you set up once.

## Set up once

1. **Open the till** on each counter with your email or phone and password. See
   [Open the till](/help/start/open-the-till).
2. **Staff**: add each person with their name, role and a 4-digit PIN. Managers can approve; cashiers
   cannot. See [Staff and PINs](/help/setup/staff-and-pins).
3. **Settings**: the shop's **UPI ID** (so the payment screen shows a QR with the amount), **GST
   registration** when the till is not linked to Inventory, and the **logo on the bill**. See
   [Settings](/help/setup/settings).
4. **Inventory link**: paste the key from Inventory, then **Refresh items from Inventory**. Items, prices,
   GST rates, stock, your shop's name, address and GSTIN come from Inventory. See
   [Inventory link](/help/behind/inventory-link).

:::note GST is your choice
With a GSTIN, the till prints **TAX INVOICE** with CGST and SGST. Without one, it prints a plain receipt
with no GST. A missing GSTIN never stops a sale. See [Which bill the till prints](/help/gst/which-bill).
:::

## Every day

- **Reports**: how the day went, and who owes. [Reports](/help/reports/reports)
- **Sales by salesperson**: for incentives. [Sales by salesperson](/help/reports/sales-by-salesperson)
- **Close the day**: once every shift is closed. [Close the day](/help/day/close-the-day)

## For your accountant

**More → Connections → Sales for your accountant → Download Excel**: every bill and credit note with its
GST, GST by rate, the HSN summary for GSTR-1, and the days. See
[The accountant's Excel](/help/reports/accountant-export).

## Common problems

:::faq A cashier forgot their PIN
A PIN cannot be read back — not even by you. Set a new one: **More → Staff**, the person, **New PIN**.
:::

:::faq I want to change a customer's GSTIN
Only an owner or manager can change a GSTIN already on a customer. Anyone can add one where there was
none. Old bills keep the GSTIN they were issued with.
:::
