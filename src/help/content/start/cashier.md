You make the bills. You do not need to know prices, GST or stock: the till knows them. Your job is to
put the right items on the bill, take the right money, and give the customer their bill.

## 1. Take the till

At the start of your turn, the screen says **Who's at the till?**

1. Tap your name.
2. Type your 4-digit PIN.

Every bill you make now carries your name as **Cashier**. When you go for lunch, tap **Switch** at the
top so the next person takes the till with their own PIN. Your bill in progress stays as it is.

:::warning Your PIN is yours
Never tell it to anyone, and never type someone else's. The bills, the drawer and the cash out are all
recorded against the name of the PIN.
:::

## 2. Make a bill

1. Scan the tag, or type the code or name in **Find an item**.
2. Add the customer if they want points or a bill in their name: **Add customer**.
3. If a salesperson showed the sarees, choose them in **Served by**.
4. Press **Take payment**, take the money, and press **Complete sale**.

Step by step: [Make a bill](/help/selling/make-a-bill).

## 3. Take the money

The customer can pay in cash, any UPI app, any card on your swiping machine, points, store credit, or a
mix of these. See [How customers can pay](/help/payments/how-customers-can-pay).

- **UPI**: type the 12-digit UTR from the customer's phone. [UPI](/help/payments/upi)
- **Card**: type the last 4 digits and the approval code from the machine's slip. [Card machine](/help/payments/card-machine)

## 4. Returns and exchanges

Find the bill (scan its QR, or search in **Bills**), press **Return** or **Exchange**, choose the pieces.
See [Take a return](/help/returns/return) and [Exchange](/help/returns/exchange).

## 5. When the till asks for a manager

Some things need a manager's yes: a big discount, a price change, selling on credit, cash out of the
drawer, writing off what is owed. The till shows **Manager approval needed**. Call your manager: they type
their PIN on your screen and press **Approve**. Nobody is signed out. See
[Manager approval](/help/setup/manager-approval).

## 6. End of your turn

Count the drawer and close the shift if the manager asks you to. See
[Shift and the drawer](/help/day/shift-and-drawer).

## Common problems

:::faq The internet went off in the middle of a bill
Finish the bill as normal. The till saves it and says **Sale saved on this till**. It sends itself when the
internet is back; nothing to do. See [When the internet drops](/help/behind/when-the-internet-drops).
:::

:::faq I typed the wrong UPI number
Before **Complete sale**: just correct it. After: tell your manager. A UTR already used on another bill is
stopped by the till, so a copy-paste mistake is caught.
:::

:::faq The customer has gone and I forgot to give the bill
Open **Bills**, find it, and press **Share** to send it on WhatsApp, or **Print**.
:::
