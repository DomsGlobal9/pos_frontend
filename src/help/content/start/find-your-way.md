## The tabs at the bottom

- **Home**: today's sales, the drawer, orders that need attention, and your recent bills.
- **Sell**: make a bill. This is where a cashier spends the day.
- **Orders**: things kept for customers, and bills where money is still owed.
- **Customers**: find a customer, see their visits, bills, points and what they owe.
- **More**: Bills, Payments to check, Shift and drawer, Close the day, Reports, Settings, Staff and the
  rest. Some of these only show for managers and the owner.

## The chip at the top

It always tells you whether your work is safe.

| What it says | What it means |
|---|---|
| **All saved · Inventory connected** | Everything is saved, and bills are reaching Inventory. |
| **All saved** | Everything is saved. This shop does not use Inventory. |
| **1 sale waiting to send** | The internet dropped; the sale is kept on this till and sends itself. |
| **All saved · Sending to Inventory has stopped** | Your bills are safe, but Inventory refused one. Tap it to see why. |
| **Saving is paused. Nothing you have entered is lost.** | The till cannot reach its server for a moment. Keep going. |

## The ? button

The **?** at the top opens help for **the screen you are on**: on the drawer screen it opens
[Shift and the drawer](/help/day/shift-and-drawer), on an order it opens what to do with orders. It opens in
a new tab, so your bill stays where it was.

:::tip A new version of the till
When the chip says **A new version is ready — it updates by itself when the till is free**, do nothing. It
updates the moment no bill is in progress.
:::
