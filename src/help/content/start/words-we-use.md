**Approval code** — the 6 letters and numbers printed on a card machine's slip for each payment. The till
asks for it with the last 4 digits of the card.

**B2B bill (business bill)** — a tax invoice to a customer with a GSTIN. It prints their name, address and
GSTIN so they can claim the GST. See [Business bills](/help/gst/business-bills).

**Bill of supply** — the bill a shop on the GST composition scheme gives. No GST is shown on it.

**Credit note** — the paper for a return: it reverses part of a bill, with its own number (CN/…).

**Day close** — the end of the trading day, after every shift is closed. See
[Close the day](/help/day/close-the-day).

**Float** — the cash in the drawer when a shift opens, for change.

**GSTIN** — the 15-character GST number of a shop or a business customer.

**HSN** — the code for the kind of goods (5208 for cotton fabric, 5007 for silk). It is printed on each
line of a tax invoice.

**Kept order** — goods kept for the customer, usually with an advance: at the tailor, for fall and pico,
or to collect later. See [Kept orders](/help/orders/kept-orders).

**Manager approval** — a manager typing their PIN on a cashier's screen to allow something a cashier
cannot do alone. See [Manager approval](/help/setup/manager-approval).

**Parked bill** — a bill put aside for a minute so the next customer can be billed. See
[Park a bill](/help/selling/park-a-bill).

**Points** — loyalty points the customer earns on what they pay; they can pay with them later. Points are
kept by Inventory.

**Served by** — the salesperson who showed the goods, for incentives. Not the cashier.

**Shift** — one person's or one session's time with the drawer, from **Open shift** to **Close shift**.

**Store credit** — money the shop owes the customer, kept by Inventory, usable as payment.

**Tax invoice** — the bill a GST-registered shop gives, with CGST and SGST (or IGST) and their rates.

**Udhaar (credit sale)** — the goods go home now, the money is paid later. See
[Sell on credit](/help/orders/sell-on-credit).

**UTR** — the 12-digit number of a UPI payment, shown in the customer's UPI app.

**Write-off** — giving up on money a customer will never pay. The bill stays as it was. See
[Write off](/help/orders/write-off).
