A manager can do everything a cashier does, and says **yes** to the things a cashier cannot do alone.

## 1. Approve on a cashier's screen

When the till shows **Manager approval needed**, walk to the till:

1. Read what is being asked — for example *Selling on credit: ₹2,000 of a ₹2,700 bill will be owed*.
2. Check or write the reason in **Why**.
3. Type your PIN in **Manager PIN** and press **Approve**.

The cashier stays signed in, and the approval is recorded with your name. What needs a manager is listed
in [Manager approval](/help/setup/manager-approval).

## 2. Open and close the drawer

- In the morning: **More → Shift and drawer**, count the cash, **Open shift**.
- During the day: **Cash in** and **Cash out** (paying the tailor, buying tea).
- At closing: **Close shift**, count every note and coin, type the total. The till then shows what it
  expected and the difference.

See [Shift and the drawer](/help/day/shift-and-drawer).

## 3. Orders and credit (udhaar)

**Orders** lists what is kept for customers and who owes money.

- **Due**: bills where money is still owed. Open one to **Take payment**, remind the customer on
  **WhatsApp**, or **Write off** what will never come.
- **Waiting / Ready**: sarees at the tailor, fall and pico, blouses.

See [Kept orders](/help/orders/kept-orders), [Sell on credit](/help/orders/sell-on-credit) and
[Write off](/help/orders/write-off).

## 4. UPI that has not been seen yet

A cashier can save a UPI as **Not confirmed yet** when the money has not shown on the shop's phone. Check
these on **More → Payments to check** against the bank statement. See
[Payments to check](/help/payments/payments-to-check).

## 5. Reports

**More → Reports** shows the day: bills, how it was paid, returns, discounts, GST, the drawer, who owes,
and sales by salesperson. See [Reports](/help/reports/reports).

## Common problems

:::faq The header says "Sending to Inventory has stopped"
Open **More → Inventory link**. It says which bill and why. See
[Inventory link](/help/behind/inventory-link).
:::

:::faq The till says a shift has been open since yesterday
Someone did not close the drawer. Count it and **Close shift**, then open today's. See
[Shift and the drawer](/help/day/shift-and-drawer).
:::
