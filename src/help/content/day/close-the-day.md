## 1. Close every shift first

Each counter's drawer is counted and closed: see [Shift and the drawer](/help/day/shift-and-drawer).

## 2. Close the day

**More → Close the day**. The page shows the day:

- **Sales** — bills and what they came to.
- **Paid in** — cash, UPI, card…
- **Given back** — refunds on returns.
- **Cash** — what each drawer should hold and what it was counted at.


![The Close the day page with the day's sales, money in, money given back and the drawers.](1-day.webp "Every shift must be closed first.")
Press **Close the day**.

## A closed day is frozen

Its figures are the ones read when it was closed. Anything that lands on that day afterwards — a sale made
offline that arrives late — shows on its own *since closing* line, instead of quietly changing figures you
already handed over.

:::note Inventory's Day Book
Inventory has its own Day Book for the whole shop. A till bill counts there on the day it was made, even if
it reached Inventory later. See Inventory's guide:
[Day Book](https://inventory.scaleezy.com/help/settings/day-book).
:::
