The drawer is counted in **shifts**. Every cash bill, cash refund, cash in and cash out goes into the
shift open on that counter.

## 1. Open a shift

**More → Shift and drawer**. Count the cash in the drawer and type it in [[1]] **Cash in the drawer now**. Last
night's count is offered — if it is right, just press **Open shift**.


![No shift open. Cash in the drawer now is marked 1 and Open shift 2.](1-open.webp "Last count is offered for you.")

:::tip Typing a different amount
Tap the box and type: the offered figure is replaced, not added to.
:::

## 2. During the day

- **Cash in** — cash put into the drawer that is not a sale (change brought from the bank).
- **Cash out** — cash taken out (paying the tailor, buying tea). Say what for. A cashier needs a manager's
  PIN.


![An open shift. Cash in is marked 1, Cash out 2 and Close shift 3.](2-during.webp "Managers and the owner also see what should be in the drawer.")

## 3. Close the shift

1. Press **Close shift**.
2. **Count the drawer**: count every note and coin, type the total in **Counted**. Or press **Count note by note**,
   type how many of each note (₹500, ₹200 … ₹10) and the coins in rupees, and the till adds them up for you.
3. Press **Close shift**.

Only now the till shows **The till expected** and the **Difference** — *Exact*, *₹63.71 short* or
*₹200 over*. A cashier does not see the expected figure before counting, so the count is honest.

If the count is off, the till asks for a note: *Coins not counted; small change box kept aside.*

## The drawer, worked out

**Should be in the drawer** = float + cash sales − cash refunds + cash in − cash out.

:::note Example
Float ₹5,000 · cash sales ₹20,899 · refunds −₹2,700 → should be ₹23,199. Counted ₹23,199: **Exact**.
:::

## Common problems

:::faq "This shift has been open since yesterday"
It was not closed last night. Count the drawer now and **Close shift**, then open today's.
:::

:::faq "1 sale on this till has not been sent yet"
A sale made while the internet was down is still on the till, so the till would expect too little cash.
Tap **Send them first**, then count.
:::

:::faq "No shift open on Counter 1"
You can still sell, but cash taken now counts in no drawer. Press **Open shift**.
:::
