## 1. Start the exchange

Open the original bill (scan its QR, or find it in **Bills**) and press **Exchange**.

## 2. What comes back

Scan the piece coming back, or set its quantity.

## 3. What goes out

In [[2]] **Find the replacement**, scan or search the new piece and **Add** it.


![The exchange screen. What comes back is marked 1 and Find the replacement 2.](1-exchange.webp "The difference is worked out as you add.")

## 4. The difference

- **New costs more**: the customer pays the difference — cash, UPI, card, points, any way.
- **New costs less**: the rest goes back to them, the same way as a return.
- **The same**: nothing to pay.

:::note Example
A ₹2,700 saree back, a ₹12,999 silk out: the customer pays ₹10,299. The bill shows *Exchange credit
₹2,700* and the payment.
:::

## 5. Finish

The till makes a **credit note** for what came back and a **new bill** for what went out, linked to each
other. Stock and Inventory are updated for both.

:::faq Points spent on the original bill
They go back as points; the money part pays towards the new bill.
:::
