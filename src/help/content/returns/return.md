## 1. Find the bill

Scan the QR on the customer's bill, or **More → Bills** and search. Open it and press **Return**.

## 2. Choose what comes back

1. Scan the piece coming back, or set **Quantity coming back** on its line. **All of it** returns the whole
   bill.
2. Choose a **Reason**: wrong size, damaged, changed mind…

The till shows what goes back: the money, and the GST taken back with it.

## 3. How the money goes back

Choose the **Refund method**: cash, UPI, card or store credit. The till only offers what fits:

- Money goes back only up to what was **paid in money**.
- What was paid with **store credit** goes back as store credit, never cash.
- What was paid with **points** goes back as points.

## 4. Approve and finish

A cashier is asked for a manager's PIN (*Cashiers need a manager for any return*). A manager or the owner
goes straight through. After the shop's return window (7 days) it is recorded as a late return, with the reason.

The till makes a **credit note** (CN/2026-27/0006) — print or share it like a bill. The pieces go back into
stock, and Inventory is told.

## Common problems

:::faq "Collect it before anything comes back"
The bill still has money owed. Collect it first, then take the return. (A written-off bill cannot be returned.)
:::

:::faq "A payment on this bill is still being checked"
A UPI marked Not confirmed yet might never have arrived. Settle it on **Payments to check** first, so
nothing is refunded that never came.
:::

:::faq "It cannot be returned or exchanged"
Part of the bill was written off as never paid. A manager sorts it out with the customer.
:::
